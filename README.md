## A Little Bit About Me

Hi, I'm Yaaseen. I have **nearly 5 years of ITSM experience** working with business users, developers and support teams in enterprise environments. That time taught me how ServiceNow really works day to day: requests, incidents, approvals, knowledge, and the process discipline behind them.

ITSM is only one part of the platform, though. So I build projects that connect it to the **other modules**, especially ITAM, ITOM and SecOps. I want a broad foundation across ServiceNow, with ITSM as my depth. I'm still learning, and these projects are how I keep growing.

**Certifications:** ServiceNow CSA · CAD · CIS-DF (Data Foundations)

## How I Think About ServiceNow

Knowing the technical terminology and where everything lives matters. But what matters more is **what you do with it**: using the platform to solve real problems and make a real business impact. I use my personal developer instance as a place to practice exactly that. Every project starts with a business problem, and the technology follows from it.
